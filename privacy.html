Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

TrueSketch ("we", "our") is a product of MehyarSoft. This policy explains what personal data we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Your name and birthdate — provided by you, to personalize your portrait sketch and reading.
  • Your words about yourself — the personality and goals you describe, which shape your reading.
  • An optional selfie — used only as a loose artistic style reference for your sketch, then deleted. Never used in marketing without your permission.
  • Your email address — to deliver your sketch and reading, send your gallery link, and (only if you opt in) send reading tips.
  • Your IP address — used only for rate limiting (one free sketch per IP) and abuse prevention. We do not build profiles from it.

How we use it

  • To generate your portrait sketch and reading, and to deliver them to you via email and your private gallery link.
  • To operate, secure, and improve the service (including preventing abuse of the free tier).
  • We do not sell your personal data. We do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.

Data retention

  • Selfies are deleted after your sketch is generated.
  • Paid order records (name, email, sketch, reading) are kept so your private gallery link keeps working — delete anytime by emailing us.
  • Free-tier inputs are kept only to enforce the one-per-IP limit.

Your choices

  • Access / deletion: email team@mehyar.us and we will export or delete your data.
  • Unsubscribe: every email we send includes a one-click unsubscribe link; you can also unsubscribe anytime at truesketch.mehyar.us/unsubscribe.

Payments

Payments are processed securely by Stripe. We never see or store your card number.

Children

TrueSketch is for entertainment and is intended for users 18 and older.

Entertainment purposes only

TrueSketch creates artistic portraits and AI-written readings for fun and self-reflection. Nothing here is psychic guidance, professional advice, or a prediction of your future.
