FAQ

Fair questions.

Will the sketch look like me?

The sketch is an artistic interpretation inspired by you — not a portrait from your photo. If you upload a selfie, we use it only as a loose style reference (hair, general vibe). We never promise likeness, and that's part of the magic: it's how the AI sees your energy.

Is the reading real? Is this fortune-telling?

No — it's entertainment. The reading is written by AI from what you tell us about yourself, in a warm and playful tone. It can be surprisingly on-point and a lovely mirror, but it's not psychic, not therapy, and not advice. For entertainment purposes only.

How long does it take?

Usually minutes. The sketch renders and the reading is written automatically right after your payment confirms. Your success page shows live progress, and your private gallery link is emailed to you.

What do you do with my selfie?

We use it once, as a style reference for your sketch, then it's deleted. We never sell data, never train on it, and never use your photo in marketing without permission.

What if I don't love it?

Email us within 7 days and we'll regenerate it once, free. Still not right? Full refund. Bad work shouldn't be your problem.

Who owns the sketch and reading?

You do. Full rights, forever. Print it, frame it, post it, gift it.
